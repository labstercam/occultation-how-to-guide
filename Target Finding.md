# Target Finding
## Or how not to be Lost In Space!!

One of the most challenging tasks for new observers is finding the target star. These are often faint, in the mag 13-16 range, and if in the milky way may be in a very crowded field.

Plate Solving is the best way to find target stars. This uses software built into SharpCap or other tools to analyse a frame and determine its exact RA/DEC coordinates, orientation (rotation) and field of view.
